Data-Software Analysis Case Study

Data Software Analysis is an IT and digital solutions company offering software development, AI and data analytics, digital marketing, SEO, IT solutions and technology services. I worked on strengthening the brand's digital presence through SEO-focused content, social media marketing, service promotion and lead-generation content. The main focus was to communicate technical services in a simple, business-friendly way while targeting both business clients and students/job seekers.

My Role

Digital Marketing Intern

    I researched keywords related to:
  1. Software Development

  3. SEO Services
  4. Data Analytics
  5. Digital Marketing Courses
  6. IT Jobs & Internships

2. Social Media Content Strategy — I created content designed to make technical IT services easier for business owners and potential customers to understand.
