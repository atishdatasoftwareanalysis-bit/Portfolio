City Spirit Roadmap

The roadmap is built around four major content themes: Founder’s POV, Faces of Nashik, Entrepreneurial Insights, and Personal Reflections/Micro-Lessons. This creates a balance between local storytelling, entrepreneurship, motivation, and personal experiences.

01

Target Audience & Platforms:

The content is designed for young professionals, entrepreneurs, students, creators, and people interested in business and personal growth. The focus is on building a strong local community around meaningful conversations.

02

4-Week Content Calendar

The strategy is divided into four weeks, with a planned mix of Reels, Posts, and Stories. Each week covers different themes such as founder journeys, local personalities, podcast insights, and motivational lessons.

03

Repurposing Podcast Content

Each podcast episode is converted into multiple social-media assets, including short reels, quotes, stories, entrepreneurial insights, and micro-lessons. This helps City_Spirit get maximum value and reach from every podcast conversation.

04

Showcasing Nashik’s People & Stories

The “Faces of Nashik” series helps introduce interesting local entrepreneurs and personalities through posts and stories. This positions City_Spirit as a platform that celebrates the people shaping Nashik. Similar local initiatives also emphasize connecting audiences with Nashik’s entrepreneurs, artists, and community members.

05

Building the City_Spirit Community

The overall goal is to position City_Spirit as a voice of Nashik’s entrepreneurial and creative community. By consistently showcasing local stories and sharing valuable insights, the roadmap aims to increase brand awareness, engagement, audience growth, and community connection.
