Grace Engineerings Case Study

Grace Engineerings is an industrial engineering and fabrication company serving businesses in Sinnar, Nashik, and surrounding industrial areas. For this project, I worked on improving the website’s SEO foundation and strengthening its visibility for relevant industrial and local search terms.

What I Did

SEO Audit & Optimization

SEO Audit — Reviewed website structure, pages, metadata, content, headings, and overall SEO setup.

Keyword Research — Researched industrial engineering and fabrication keywords focused on Sinnar, Nashik, and nearby areas.

Keyword Mapping — Mapped target keywords to relevant website pages and search intent.

On-Page SEO — Optimized titles, meta descriptions, H1/H2 headings, content structure, image alt text, and keyword placement.

Service Page Optimization — Improved content for industrial fabrication, structural steel fabrication, industrial sheds, PEB structures, and related services.

Local SEO — Targeted location-based searches related to Sinnar and Nashik.

Competitor Analysis — Analyzed competing industrial and fabrication websites to identify keyword opportunities and content gaps.

Content Optimization — Improved website content to make it more informative, relevant, and search-engine friendly.

Technical SEO — Identified structural improvements supporting better crawling, indexing, and website performance.

SEO Strategy — Developed a keyword and optimization strategy focused on attracting relevant industrial customers through organic search.
