Dnyanada career academy Case Study

Dnyanada Career Academy is an educational and career-focused institution dedicated to helping students develop their knowledge, skills, and career opportunities. As part of my SEO project, I worked on keyword research and website optimization to improve its online visibility, target relevant search queries, and enhance Google rankings for selected keywords.

My Role

SEO & Keyword research

1. SEO & Keyword Research — I conducted keyword research for Dnyanada Career Academy to identify relevant search terms related to its courses, educational programs and career-focused services. I used these keywords to optimise website content and improve its relevance to students searching for educational and career opportunities.

    I researched keywords related to:
  1. Career Academy & Educational Courses
  2. Competitive Exam Preparation
  3. Location-Based Keywords

2. Website SEO Optimization — I used the keyword research to support website optimization and align page content with relevant search queries. My focus was to make the website more search-engine-friendly and improve its potential to attract students through organic search.

    My SEO activities included:
  1. On-Page SEO Recommendations
  2. SEO-Friendly Content Optimization
  3. Search Intent Analysis

Keyword Ranking & Performance — I monitored the progress of targeted keywords and observed that some keywords continued to perform in search results after the initial SEO work. This helped demonstrate the potential long-term value of keyword research and website optimization.

    My key focus areas were:
  1. Tracking Targeted Keyword Performance
  2. Identifying Keywords with Ranking Potential
  3. Improving Website Search Visibility
  4. Supporting Long-Term Organic Traffic Growth
